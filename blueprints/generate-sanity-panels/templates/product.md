# Product page spine

Brief-agnostic **buyer-intent** default sequence. Adapt per `{brief}` — omit a beat only when findings do not support it.

1. **Hero** — what the product is + why it matters (product clarity + benefit; intrigue may support, never replace)
2. **Why read / who it's for** — pathway tiles or highlight ribbon (buying guidance first)
3. **Key themes** — tile grid with short labels (scannable, not editorial essay)
4. **Comparison** — works and/or format chooser (`What it is` / `Best for` / `Skip if`)
5. **Gift suitability** — when gift persona is in play
6. **Social proof** — only when attributed findings exist (no invented ratings)
7. **FAQ** — product-fit objections not already answered (no store returns/dispatch policy)
8. **Related / closing CTA** — single outbound maker-catalogue or browse path

**Module budget:** 6–9 blocks. **Every block:** `sectionTitle` + `sectionSubtitle` per `page-structure.md`.

**Maker** = author (books/Bibles/media) or manufacturer/brand (candles, instruments, gifts, etc.). Adapt labels to product type.

No fixed panel `_type`s here. Content stages name sections only. **Content strip only** — do not duplicate live PDP chrome (title, price, buy box, core specs table).

**Banned on product strips:** `featuredOrganisationJobs`, `organisationJobSearch`, `appJobs`, `markdown` / `richText` panel types.
