# Definition — article

Delta for `target=article`.

- No extra requirements — narrative `{brief}` is enough
- Identifier = slug, doc id, or topic-only brief (optional; not blocking)
- `panelField` = `richText[]` embeds
- Template spine: Hero → 8–12 content panels → CTA (default `templates/article.md`)
- Algolia optional — author or topic hunt in stage 4
