# Research Strategy — product

Topics must serve the **content strip** (panels below live PDP chrome), not restate buy-box / spec chrome.

## Always consider (when brief + catalogue support)

- **Maker spotlight** — author **or** manufacturer/brand by product type; childhood/origin, faith or brand values link, why they matter for **this** SKU
- **Imprint / brand** — publisher or manufacturer standing (not marketplace buy-box chrome)
- **Themes / use cases** — what it is about; personal, church, gift, institutional
- **Audience & institutional fit** — derive from category/audience signals. Credited sources only; no fake endorsements
- **Social proof** — credited reviews, endorsements, press, or awards the agent discovers; do **not** prescribe named retailers or review sites as layout models — keep source choice open
- **FAQ / customer questions** — review themes, common buyer questions, format confusion (HB vs ebook), gift suitability — from trusted discoverable sources
- **Related catalogue** — same maker/brand siblings for commerce panels (titles, formats, URLs, covers). Stock/dispatch from Algolia is for **gate/selection only** — never quote on-page
- **Differentiation** — choose-or-skip vs close alternatives
- **SEO / FAQ intents** — entity clarity for maker + title + use

## Specs topic — conditional

Add a specs topic **only if** content strip needs facts **not** already on live PDP spec/chrome. Otherwise skip.

## Do not default to “author”

If seed product has no meaningful author (candles, instruments, etc.), use manufacturer/brand topics instead.
